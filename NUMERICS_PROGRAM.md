## Addendum to [2] --- the Palm insertion route, in detail (decided 2026-07-05)

**Decision.** The first MC detection of an odd-k connected correlator runs
through Palm conditioning, not the third joint cumulant. Since the equal-time
field is Poisson (statics), conditioning on walls at prescribed positions at
time 0 equals INSERTING them into an independent equilibrium sample (exact at
finite N: iid bonds conditioned even on the remaining bonds; two inserted
walls preserve torus parity; on the open chain no parity constraint at all).
The conditional intensity profile at (x, tau) given walls pinned at y1, y2 is

    rho(x,tau | y1,y2) = 1 + f(x-y1,tau) + f(x-y2,tau) + f3((y1,0),(y2,0),(x,tau)),

and the deviation from the superposition baseline is the equal-time-pinned
three-point loop -- POINTWISE in the pinned arguments (no smearing dilution;
only the probe is box-averaged). Any real/determinantal kernel predicts a
deviation identically zero, so sign, magnitude and shape are all sharp tests.

**Why it wins.** The equal-time pinned block K(D, 0) has O(1) entries
(~4 e^{-2D}), and two of the three points need no smearing; the kappa3 route
smears all three points over boxes and pays cumulant-estimator variance.

optimum; sign NEGATIVE between the walls throughout):
